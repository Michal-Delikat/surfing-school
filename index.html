01 / Sessions

Choose your session.

01

First Wave

Who it's for: complete beginners, or anyone coming back to surfing after a long break. You need to be able to swim; no surf experience is required.

  1. Meet at the Barrel hut, fit a soft-top and rashguard, then check the forecast and currents.
  2. Watch the water for five minutes, warm up and practise the paddle stroke and pop-up on the sand.
  3. Enter the water with the instructor and work on paddling, catching whitewater and your first take-offs.
  4. Finish with one uncoached wave, then a short debrief and one clear goal for next time.

2 h · max 4 · beginner-friendly

38 USD per person

02

Next Step

Who it's for: surfers who can paddle out and stand up on whitewater, and who can swim comfortably in chest-deep water.

  1. Meet at the hut, match your board to the conditions and read the forecast, currents and peak.
  2. Watch the line-up and plan your entry, paddle route and position.
  3. Paddle out with the instructor and work on positioning, take-off timing and riding unbroken waves.
  4. Take the last wave uncoached, then get feedback and one clear task for your next session.

2 h · max 3 · intermediate

45 USD per person

03

Private 1:1

Who it's for: anyone from a first-timer to an experienced surfer who wants individual attention. One student, one instructor.

  1. Start with a short briefing about your experience, goals and the current conditions.
  2. Choose the warm-up and drills that match what you want to work on.
  3. Get in the water for focused coaching — from your first pop-up to unbroken waves and turns.
  4. Finish with a debrief and a plan you can take into your next session.

2 h · 1 person · all levels

55 USD per session

Every booking is one two-hour session — no multi-day packages. Included: soft-top board, rashguard, wax, drinking water, reef-safe sunscreen, hotel pickup within Canggu and accident insurance. Airport transfer is not included. Prices are in USD. We don't promise waves or a stand-up — we promise coaching, safety and time in the water.

02 / How it works

From the sand to your first wave.

  1. 01

    Book your session

    Choose a session and a date. You'll get an email with the meeting time at the Barrel hut and a short packing list. We start at 06:30, 09:00 or — in the dry season — 15:30.

  2. 02

    Meet at the Barrel hut

    A bamboo shed on the sand, a short walk from Pura Batu Bolong. We fit your board and rashguard and check the forecast, currents and bottom.

  3. 03

    Five minutes watching the water

    No boards. Then a warm-up, paddle drill and pop-up on the sand. We show you where to enter and how to read the wave at Batu Bolong.

  4. 04

    Into the water

    The instructor stays close and uses hand signals, no shouting. First paddle, first take-off, one clear task for the next session. The last wave is yours — no coaching.

03 / Why Barrel

Less show. More water.

01

An instructor in the water

Four instructors, all ISA-certified with lifeguard and first-aid training. One leads no more than four people and stays with the group, not on the beach with a megaphone.

02

Crowded? We change beaches

Batu Bolong can get busy — around 90 people in the water on weekends. That's why the first session starts at 06:30, and when it's crowded we move to Berawa or Pererenan.

03

Gear with no extras

Soft-top board, rashguard, wax, drinking water and reef-safe sunscreen are included. You won't need a wetsuit — the water stays at 26–29 °C all year.

04

Safety without improvising

We check the forecast, currents and bottom. The shore pound at Batu Bolong can be heavy, so we enter and exit with an instructor. In poor conditions we move the session.

“Barrel isn't a goal. It's something you watch from the shore at Echo Beach — and come back for once you've found your feet.”

06 / Practical info

Before you get in the water.

Canggu, Bali Barrel hut — fictional location

Getting here

From Ngurah Rai airport (DPS), the drive to Canggu depends heavily on traffic — mornings and evenings can add a lot to the journey. Check the current travel time before you leave. We meet at the Barrel hut, a short walk from Pura Batu Bolong.

What to bring

  • Swimwear and a towel
  • Reef-safe sunscreen
  • A water bottle
  • We provide the board, rashguard and wax

Cancellations and weather

Free reschedule or refund up to 24 hours before the session. If we call off a session because of conditions, you can choose another date, another day or get a refund.

Water and gear

The water stays at 26–29 °C all year. No wetsuit needed — a rashguard is enough. We match boards to your level and the conditions.

Tourist levy

Foreign visitors pay a one-off IDR 150,000 (about USD 10) online via the official Love Bali portal or at the airport. It is separate from the visa. We don't give visa advice — check official sources.

Session photos

On request. Tell us when you book and the instructor will take a camera into the water. We can't promise a photo of every wave, but we'll bring back what we can.

07 / FAQ

Questions we hear most often.

hello@barrel.example

Which session should I book?

Start with First Wave if you've never surfed. Choose Next Step if you can already paddle out and stand up on whitewater. Book Private 1:1 if you want individual coaching at any level.

I've never stood on a board. Is this for me?

Yes. The First Wave session assumes zero experience. The day-one goal is one stand-up on your own — the barrel can wait, best watched from the shore at Echo Beach.

Do I need to be a strong swimmer?

Yes, swimming ability is required. We stay close to the shore with an instructor alongside the group. Pregnant guests and anyone over 60 should write to us first — we'll advise whether a session makes sense.

How many people are in a group?

Up to four people on a First Wave session and up to three on Next Step. Private 1:1 is one student with one instructor.

What's included?

Soft-top board, rashguard, wax, drinking water, reef-safe sunscreen, hotel pickup within Canggu and accident insurance. Session photos on request. Airport transfer is not included.

What if the weather is bad?

If we call off a session because of conditions, you get another date, another day or a refund. Please tell us about a cancellation or reschedule at least 24 hours before the start.

What language are lessons in?

English and Bahasa Indonesia. Instructions in the water are short and based on hand signals, so language isn't a barrier.

What's the minimum age?

Eight. Anyone under 12 needs a parent or guardian on the beach. Write to us in advance if you have specific needs — we'll work out a plan.

Can I bring my own board?

Yes, just let us know when you book. For your first sessions we recommend our soft-top — loose longboards at Batu Bolong can be a real hazard.

Will it be crowded?

Batu Bolong can have around 90 people in the water on weekends. That's why we start at 06:30, cap groups at four and move to Berawa or Pererenan when it's busy.

When's the best time to visit?

The dry season runs from April to October, and mornings usually have the cleanest conditions and a light offshore wind. In the wet season the waves are smaller and mellower — good for first sessions — but water quality drops after heavy rain. The biggest crowds come in July and December.

Will I definitely stand up?

We don't promise waves or a stand-up. We promise coaching, safety and time in the water — the rest depends on the day, the water and your work on the board.

Do I have to pay the tourist levy?

Yes. Foreign visitors pay a one-off IDR 150,000 (about USD 10) online via the Love Bali portal or at the airport. It is separate from the visa. We don't give visa advice — check official sources.
