Sessions

Choose your session.

First Wave

Complete beginners, or anyone coming back to surfing after a long break. You need to be able to swim; no surf experience is required.

  1. Each session starts at the hut, where you get a board and a rashguard that fit.
  2. We spend the first half hour on the sand: warm-up, paddling, the pop-up and how to fall safely.
  3. Then we walk in, and for the next hour and a half your instructor stays beside you, lining you up on each wave and giving one correction at a time.
  4. Back on the beach there is a rinse and fresh fruit.

2 h · max 4 · beginner-friendly

38 USD per person

Next Step

Surfers who can paddle out and stand up on whitewater, and who can swim comfortably in chest-deep water.

  1. Each session starts at the hut, where we check the conditions and pick the spot.
  2. We spend a few minutes on the sand on one thing to work on, like your bottom turn or take-off timing.
  3. Then we paddle out, you choose your own waves, and your instructor gives you a note between sets.
  4. We finish with a short debrief on the beach.

2 h · max 3 · intermediate

45 USD per person

Private 1:1

Anyone from a first-timer to an experienced surfer who wants individual attention. One student, one instructor.

  1. Start with a short briefing about your experience, goals and the current conditions.
  2. Choose the warm-up and drills that match what you want to work on.
  3. Get in the water for focused coaching — from your first pop-up to unbroken waves and turns.
  4. Finish with a debrief and a plan you can take into your next session.

2 h · 1 person · all levels

55 USD per session

Each booking is a single two-hour session, priced in USD. It includes a board that fits your level, a rashguard, wax, drinking water, reef-safe sunscreen and accident insurance.

FAQ

Questions we hear most often.

hello@barrel.example

Canggu, Bali Barrel hut — fictional location
Which session should I book?

Start with First Wave if you've never surfed. Choose Next Step if you can already paddle out and stand up on whitewater. Book Private 1:1 if you want individual coaching at any level.

Do I need to be a strong swimmer?

Yes, swimming ability is required. We stay close to the shore with an instructor alongside the group.

What if the weather is bad?

If we call off a session because of conditions, you get another date, another day or a refund. Please tell us about a cancellation or reschedule at least 24 hours before the start.

What language are lessons in?

English and Bahasa Indonesia.

What's the minimum age?

18. Clients aged 14+ must come with a parent or legal guardian.

Can I bring my own board?

Yes, just let us know when you book. For your first sessions we recommend our soft-top — loose longboards at Batu Bolong can be a real hazard.

Will it be crowded?

Batu Bolong can have around 90 people in the water on weekends. That's why we start at 06:30, cap groups at four and move to Berawa or Pererenan when it's busy.

When's the best time to visit?

The dry season runs from April to October, and mornings usually have the cleanest conditions and a light offshore wind. In the wet season the waves are smaller and mellower — good for first sessions — but water quality drops after heavy rain. The biggest crowds come in July and December.
